## Design Context

### Users
The business owner operates the admin dashboard to assign physical NFC items and manage customers. Customers need a clear, low-friction way to activate accounts, edit a private profile draft and publish it. Public visitors tap or scan an item to connect with a professional.

### Brand Personality
Clean, modern, professional. Quiet confidence and trust, consistent with PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the existing Geist, amber, charcoal and restrained light/dark dashboard language. Avoid generic blue SaaS styling, decorative gradients in dashboards, excessive shadows and playful ornament. Public profiles may retain customer-selected themes.

### Design Principles
1. Efficiency for the operator; clarity for the customer.
2. Distinguish private draft, saved revision and published revision unmistakably.
3. Amber identifies primary actions and meaningful active states.
4. Use accessible native controls, visible focus and responsive layouts.
5. Preserve coherent components and spacing rather than redesigning unrelated screens.
