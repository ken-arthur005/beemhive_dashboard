## Design Context

### Users
The business owner operates the admin dashboard to assign physical NFC items and manage customers. Customers need a clear, low-friction way to activate accounts, edit a private profile draft and publish it. Public visitors tap or scan an item to connect with a professional.

### Brand Personality
Premium, warm, expressive, professional. Beem Hive should make customers proud of their digital identity and feel memorable to clients. The creator approved a full visual makeover on 2026-10-11.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Honey & Ink.** Blend public/inspo2.jpg's airy, warm composition with public/inspo1.jpg's dimensional cards and selective translucent surfaces. Ivory and honey in light mode; warm ink and smoked surfaces in dark mode. Manrope headings, DM Sans interface text, Geist Mono for URLs. Customer top navigation; theme-matched admin sidebar. Public profiles retain customer-selected backgrounds and share presentation with draft previews. Use purposeful gradients, soft depth, arched portraits, pill actions, and asymmetrical layouts. Never use decorative little icons inside pastel rounded-square tiles.

### Design Principles
1. Efficiency for the operator; clarity for the customer.
2. Distinguish private draft, saved revision and published revision unmistakably.
3. Honey identifies actions and active states and anchors the digital-identity composition.
4. Design for desktop and everyday phones; use accessible controls, readable contrast and visible focus.
5. Expressive GSAP motion: a signature bee flight/logo reveal, page choreography, a sliding nav indicator, and dimensional NFC cards. Respect reduced motion, clean up on unmount, and keep forms usable immediately.
6. Default customer landing is My Profile until first publication, then Overview. Retained publication history makes the rule durable after unpublishing. Preserve explicit, safe deep links.
7. Real account data only; loading, error and empty states are part of the design.
